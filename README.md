# Takura Overseas Gateway

Build a modern, professional, trustworthy corporate website for Takura Overseas Pvt. Ltd., an ISO 9001:2015 certified foreign employment / international recruitment agency based in Nepal. The site must feel credible, corporate, and internationally trustworthy - like a company that places skilled Nepali workers with major global employers (hotels like Marriott/Holiday Inn/Crowne Plaza, Qatar Airways, McDonald's, Unilever, etc.). Avoid anything that looks like a generic template - this is a licensed government-regulated agency, so polish, legitimacy signals, and clarity matter more than flashy effects.

Brand Identity
Company name: Takura Overseas (full legal name: Takura Overseas Pvt. Ltd.)
Tagline: "Creating a better future"
Logo: A stylized red "T" mark (angular/ribbon-like, sharp geometric flag-shape) above the wordmark "TAKURA" in bold black uppercase letters, with "overseas" in small black lowercase letters underneath, tracked out with letter-spacing. Use this exact logo lockup in the header (top-left) and footer.
Color palette (strict - white, black, red only, no other accent colors):
Primary Red:
#ED1C24 (vivid red - used for the logo mark, CTAs, section accents, icons, underlines)
Deep Charcoal/Black:
#242424 (headings, dark section backgrounds, footer)
White:
#FFFFFF (base background)
Light Grey:
#F5F5F5 (alternating section backgrounds, cards)
Optional supporting neutral: mid-grey
#6B6B6B for body text
Typography: A clean, bold, modern sans-serif (e.g., Poppins, Inter, or Montserrat) - bold/extra-bold weight for headings (uppercase style like the brochure headers "ABOUT US", "WHAT WE DO"), regular weight for body copy. Keep generous line-height and letter-spacing on all-caps headers to match the brochure's typographic style.
Visual motifs: Large soft-edged overlapping circles/blobs in red and dark grey as decorative background shapes (as seen throughout the brochure) - use these behind hero sections and to break up content blocks. Rounded rectangle "chips"/pill badges in red for numbered steps and category tags. A thin red horizontal underline beneath section headings.
Imagery style: Professional, real-world photography of workers/industries - construction workers in hard hats and safety vests, factory/manufacturing floors, oil & gas industrial sites, hospitality staff at hotel receptions, healthcare professionals, drivers/transportation, office handshake/meeting photography. Use duotone red/grey overlay treatments on hero images (like the cover page's silhouette-over-industrial-photo effect) for a premium look.
